Sumble

A daily number puzzle for iPhone.

Six numbers. One target. Combine them with + − × ÷ until you land on it exactly. You don't have to use every number, and fewer moves is better.

Everyone in the world gets the same puzzle each day, and every weekday plays a little differently — some days the division button is switched off, some days the numbers are large, Sundays take five moves.

Four ways to play

  • Daily — one puzzle a day, build a streak
  • Practice — endless puzzles, three difficulties
  • Rush — how many can you solve in two minutes?
  • Challenge — send a friend a code and you both get the same puzzle

Good to know

  • Plays offline — puzzles are generated on your phone
  • No account, no sign-up
  • Home screen widget with today's target
  • Game Center leaderboards
  • Free, with ads — one purchase removes them for good
